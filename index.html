CYBERSECURITY ADVISORY

Defending Against Deepfake Fraud

  • Verify Out-of-Band: Always confirm urgent wire transfers or voice requests via an independent trusted phone number.
  • Probabilistic Evidence: An Anomaly Index measures signal artifacts—never treat automated scores as sole legal proof.
  • Metadata Context: Social media platforms routinely strip EXIF and C2PA credentials during re-encoding; missing metadata alone does not prove manipulation.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & LIMITATIONS

About DeepGuard AI

DeepGuard AI is a multimodal digital forensics and media verification platform engineered to detect synthetic media, cloned speech, manipulated imagery, and AI-generated text using explainable signal-level metrics.

Privacy-First Zero-Upload Design

All frame extraction, FFT spectral analysis, Error Level Analysis, SHA-256 hashing, and PDF generation execute locally inside your browser sandbox. Sensitive personal videos and voice recordings are never transmitted to public servers.

Model Evaluation & Limitations

Signal heuristics (such as >7.5 kHz vocoder cutoffs, ELA compression variance, and facial frame deltas) can be affected by heavy social-media recompression, low lighting, or background noise. Always combine automated findings with human source verification.
